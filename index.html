03 Research

Research

Multi-Sensor Fusion UAV Tracking System

An EKF-based UAV tracking system combining RF, radar, acoustic, and optical sensors with a reported 12.5% improvement in tracking accuracy.

Python EKF Sensor Fusion UAV Tracking Computer Vision
View Research

This research project developed a multi-sensor fusion framework using Extended Kalman Filtering for UAV tracking. Four sensor modalities — RF, radar, acoustic, and optical — were combined to achieve a reported 12.5% improvement in tracking accuracy. Published in CEUR Workshop Proceedings as part of DoSIER 2024.
